A little about me.

Hey! I’m Nireeksha, a Computer Science Engineering undergraduate at Sapthagiri NPS University, Bengaluru.

I’m interested in the intersection of design and technology. I enjoy creating visually appealing and user-friendly interfaces, designing digital experiences in Figma and Canva, and converting those ideas into functional websites using modern frontend technologies.

I love experimenting with ideas, participating in hackathons, contributing to communities and learning new technologies. From designing event creatives to building websites and exploring AI-powered applications, I enjoy working on things that combine creativity with problem-solving.

I believe good design should not just look beautiful; it should make people’s experiences simpler, more intuitive and enjoyable.
